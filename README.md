# TechSrijan:-

The official website for **TechSrijan**, the annual techno-management festival of **Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), Gorakhpur**.

TechSrijan brings together students, innovators, creators, and technology enthusiasts through technical competitions, workshops, exhibitions, and management events. It is coordinated by MMMUT’s Technical Sub-Council and welcomes participation from institutions across India.

The website is currently under construction.
